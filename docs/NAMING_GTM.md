## Positioning (lock this)

**Category we own:** Contest-to-hire workspace — workers compete by shipping; one winner gets paid and becomes employee; sponsors and plugins turn the win into production.

**Not:** Upwork (hourly marketplace). Not 99designs-only (creative contests that end at a file). Not a pure DAO jobs board.

**One-liner (working):**  
*Lock the payout. Ship the work. Hire the winner.*

**Money story (external):**

## Beachhead ICP (first 90 days)

**Primary buyer:** Solo founder / small studio lead who needs **one concrete deliverable this week** (landing page, bot, short video, packshot, script) and might keep the winner on a seat.

**Why them:** High willingness to escrow full payout; hates interview theater; already uses Discord/Telegram/crypto rails; will try a custodial-sim then on-chain.

**Not first:** Enterprise procurement, pure commodity gigs ($20 logos), open-ended retainers.

**Beachhead job type (GTM, not schema):** **Automation / small shipping bots & scripts** — matches Dante’s stack (crypto, web3, automation) and makes “job-scoped bots + IDE/repo plugins” demo-real. General submissions still work; marketing speaks automation first.

**Secondary wedge:** Creative stills/video where sponsors (PoD, print, ads) care about fulfillment later.

## Go-to-market sequence

### Phase 0 — Closed alpha (now → stub polish)
- Invite-only workspaces (link jobs, no public feed — already in v1 cut).
- 10–20 employer friends + crypto/builder Discords.
- Success metric: escrow funded jobs / week, winner selected %, seat conversion intent (even if unpaid).

### Phase 1 — “Ship for escrow” launch
- Narrative: *Lock pay. Compete by shipping. One hire.*
- Content: 3 public demo jobs with real (small) escrow on custodial; record the read-only review UX.
- Fee transparency: “2.5% only when you hire the winner. Refunds if nobody wins.”
- Channel: builder Twitter/X, Indie Hackers, relevant Discords, one crypto jobs dump — not LinkedIn spray.

### Phase 2 — Sponsor wedge
- One financing or software sponsor per alpha workspace (IDE/repo seats).
- Placement fee when custodial live; materials/PoD later.
- Metric: % of escrow co-funded; sponsor-sourced employers.

### Phase 3 — Seat ARR
- Turn employment stub into $79/mo default seat (per MONEY_MODEL.md) after watching conversion.
- Bundle: seat + repo/IDE plugin.

### Phase 4 — On-chain rail marketing
- Same UX, wallet fund path; “same 2.5%”.
- Don’t lead with chain until custodial trust loop is boring.

## Open product decisions (need Dante call)

Both Make Money and Genius flagged these — draft proposed rules, then Dante picks:

1. **Nested fee rule** — Proposed default from Genius (awaiting draft formalization): fee (**250 bps**) only when money **leaves the company tree to a winner payee**; internal carve-outs at **0 bps**; expansion locks create a new fee base on their outward releases.
2. **Cancel / refund cascade** — Deterministic rules when parent cancels or child refunds while siblings/parent still open.
3. **Sub-team fund & winner authority** — Who may post/fund/pick-winner on sub-team jobs; where seats/billing roll up (company root).
4. **Depth cap** — Max nesting for sub-teams and jobs in v1 (Genius suggested ~3).

See also `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` for working drafts from Make Money + Genius.
